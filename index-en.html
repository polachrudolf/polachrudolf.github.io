Dear reader

Decades ago, I decided to do something for readers I had never seen and never would see. I would write a book about the universe for them. It seemed simple. Many beautiful books were being published, but they were mostly popular works, more current than rooted in ancient knowledge. So I searched for the words of the authors themselves.

The beginnings were difficult. Literature grew faster than my knowledge of the universe. Familiar thinkers referred to even older works about people, the heavens, and deities — works carved in stone or preserved in memory. I looked for reports from the oldest human settlements, where generations passed on knowledge and experience, shaping it into the form we know today. It is clear that generations did not pass on anything automatically; it was individuals with extraordinary memory and literacy who cared more about moral messages to their people than about the universe. Many ideas of the thinkers I could not reach for factual or time reasons; I had to rely on commentaries.

As everyone has their favourite book, so do I: Aristotle’s *On the Soul*, Plato’s *Laws*, the *Book of the Dead* from the Pyramid Texts, Spinoza’s wonderful *Treatise on the Emendation of the Intellect*, Kant’s *Prolegomena*, Feynman’s *The Character of Physical Law*, and Smullyan’s *Forever Undecided*. You will find several quotations from them in the books I offer you, dear reader.

Author
